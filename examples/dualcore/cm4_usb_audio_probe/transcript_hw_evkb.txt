EVKB clean run -- 2026-08-07. THE ORACLE for Phase 7.3. The Cortex-M4 drives
an ISOCHRONOUS USB AUDIO STREAM: claims the device, negotiates a format, runs
the control sequence, arms the siTD ring in OCRAM2 and streams -- AUDIBLY.

Built -DPROBE_RATE_HZ=44100 (the QEMU default of 48000 is the emulated
device's only rate; this dongle is 44.1k). Dongle 1B3F:2008 on J47, full
speed. Operator confirmed a TONE IN THE HEADPHONES, which is the un-fakeable
part: no counter can fake air moving.

  pkts=8109  xerr=0  unders=0  queued=3884  over elapsed=4000 ms
  lps=142332 loops/s

★ THE LATENCY QUESTION IS ANSWERED, and it was the phase's real risk.
Measured on the CM7 at 996 MHz (usb_audio_duplex_test/transcript_hw_cpu_budget
.txt), the USB path's genuine work is only ~1.16% of the core, but the binding
constraint is how long you can go WITHOUT calling service(): 600 us stalls soak
clean, 850 us fail. The open worry was whether a 400 MHz M4 with a simpler
pipeline could hold that.

  142,332 loops/s = a service pass every ~7 us.

That is roughly 85x margin against the 600 us figure. The CM4 is not close to
the edge, and 7.4 can add graph work without the transport being the thing
that breaks first. xerr=0 and unders=0 over the window corroborate it.

☆ ONE NUMBER IS NOT YET EXPLAINED, and it is recorded rather than smoothed
over. 8109 packets in 4000 ms is ~2027/s, but a FULL-SPEED device has a
physically fixed 1000 frames/s, and the CM7 reports exactly 1000/s from the
same counter (usb_audio_duplex_test heartbeat, OUT[pkts/s=1000]).

What packets_sent actually counts is the key: it increments when
sitd_fill_out() ARMS a ring slot (usb_audio.cpp:1310), not when a frame is
transmitted. So the candidate explanation is double-ARMING -- the CM4's much
faster service loop revisiting a slot the controller has retired but not yet
re-run -- rather than double-TRANSMITTING.

Two things point that way rather than at a genuine 2x wire rate:
  - queued=3884 sits near the FIFO's 4096 ceiling. A stream consuming frames
    at 2x would drain the FIFO and trip unders; unders=0.
  - the tone was audible and the operator did not report it sounding fast or
    high-pitched, which a 2x sample rate would make obvious.

NOT CLAIMED EITHER WAY. The next step is a delta measurement against a known
wall-clock interval on both cores with the same build, which is what would
settle it. Recorded here so it is not mistaken later for a transport finding.
