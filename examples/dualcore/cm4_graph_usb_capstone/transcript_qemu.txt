QEMU gate transcript for cm4_graph_usb_capstone (Phase 7.4, the CAPSTONE),
captured 2026-08-07. Stable 3x: every deterministic token below is
byte-identical across runs; only the timing-derived ones (dwt, gcyc, gcycmax,
cpu, loops, lps, elapsed) move, for the reason the budget: caveat gives.

WHAT THIS PROVES, and it is the control plane PLUS the graph:
  - the CM4 image links AudioStream.cpp, AudioOutputUSBHost, a sine source and
    a peak analyser ON TOP of 7.3's entire USB host + UAC stack, and boots;
  - the UAC driver on the M4 claims QEMU's usb-audio (46F4:0002), negotiates
    alt 1 and completes the control sequence with ctrl=0/0/0;
  - rate=48000 was set by the ADAPTER's constructor, not by the sketch --
    main_cm4.cpp never calls format();
  - the GRAPH RAN ON THE M4 and its blocks reached the USB FIFO: gblocks=3
    passes filled it to fifo=768 = fifotgt, with drop=0;
  - and the blocks CARRIED AUDIO: peak=16384 (q15) against the graph's
    requested amplitude 0.5. Nothing else in this transcript can tell a sine
    from silence -- see transcript_qemu_red_silent.txt, where fifo still
    reaches 768/768;
  - the pool is bounded at mem=1/1 (one live block, which is the declaration
    -order consequence documented in main_cm4.cpp) and prio shows usb135=0
    outranking graph44=208.

WHAT IT DELIBERATELY DOES NOT PROVE. pkts=0 and gpasses=0, and both are
CORRECT here. Isochronous data does not flow against QEMU's emulated
usb-audio (docs/KNOWN-BROKEN-GATES.md), and because the graph's clock IS
packet flow -- USBAudioOut calls the adapter back once per frame it arms --
no packets means no graph ticks either. STREAM_PACKETS, GRAPH_CLOCKED,
TRANSPORT_CLEAN and STREAM_FED are world-split: presence-checked here,
value-asserted only on silicon.
