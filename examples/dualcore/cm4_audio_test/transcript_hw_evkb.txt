builds; the CM7 side differs only in ResetHandler (0x182 -> 0x1d6) and the new
startup_ccr_at_reset global, and the CM7 here only stages the CM4 and relays MU
tokens from ITCM before parking in WFI.  The same pipeline that passed in July
starves its output graph once per block today whichever CM7 core stages it.
What differs from July: the mic is SATURATED (~0.85 FS vs 0.014 FS -- the loud
1 kHz output couples into it; the 2026-07-22 rx-overflow FAIL also read
mic_peak=0x6C85), and the SAI1 ISR fires 144 times per block instead of 128 --
16 extra entries per block, one every 8 frames.  fef=0, rx_overflows=0, FFT bin
6 / mag 0xFF2, codec_ack=1, pll_prearm=1: every deterministic token unchanged.

BENCH TRAP: this image parks the CM7 in WFI ~4 s after reset, and a WFI-parked
CM7 is INVISIBLE to LinkServer -- every `flash ... load` fails with
`Ep(03). Invalid ID for processor` (CpuID 00000FFF behind a valid DpID 6BA02477),
for the EVKB and CM7-only profiles, after a board power cycle and after a
DEBUG-USB replug alike.  It is NOT the MCU-Link DAP wedge.  The PRE image was
written by a retry loop of `flash ... load` while SW4 was pressed every ~3 s: the
loop caught the awake window on attempt 47.  The roadmap's SDP-mode boot
(SW1-3 OFF / SW1-4 ON) is the other way past it.
