EVKB clean run -- 2026-08-07. THE ORACLE. The CM4 brings up the USB host port
itself and takes USB OTG2's IRQ 135 on its own NVIC, on real silicon.

Procedure: flashed with J47 EMPTY, console reader attached, SW4/RESET pressed,
then a USB audio dongle physically plugged into J47 during the 8 s observation
window. The plug is the un-fakeable part -- a readback of registers the CM4
itself wrote would be circular (the ssi-loopback trap from Phase 3.1), so the
assertion needs BOTH halves below, from the same run.

  irqcnt=00000006      the CM4's OWN handler ran, six times
  ccs=0 -> ccs2=1      a real 0->1 connect edge, i.e. the plug actually happened
                       inside the window rather than before or after it

Neither alone would do: irqcnt could be a latched or spurious line, and a CCS
change alone says nothing about the NVIC.

Six entries rather than one is expected and is itself corroboration -- a
human pushing a connector home produces contact bounce, and each bounce is
another port-change event. A cleanly asserted single interrupt would be more
suspicious, not less.

WHAT ELSE THIS RUN SETTLES:

  pllsic=80F03040   bit 31 LOCK SET -> PHY_PLL_CM4=PASS. This is the
                    world-split token, and it reads FAIL in QEMU because stock
                    TYPE_IMX_USBPHY has no PLL_SIC register at 0xA0 at all.
                    ** It also refutes the phase's biggest open risk: Phase 6
                    finding 1 (the CM4 hangs on ANATOP's AI-write handshake)
                    does NOT apply here, because USBPHY2's PLL is plain MMIO.
                    The CM4 drove it directly, first try. **

  rstspin=00000002  the EHCI reset took two poll iterations to clear on
                    silicon, against 0 in QEMU -- the model completes the
                    reset instantly. Harmless, but it is a real divergence
                    and is recorded rather than absorbed.

  portsc=1C001000   the port-status word differs from QEMU's 00001000 in its
                    upper bits (PTS/PSPD speed and transceiver fields), which
                    the model does not populate. CCS (bit 0) is what the gate
                    reads and it agrees in both worlds.

  stsraw=00000088   post-window raw USBSTS: bits 3 (frame-list rollover) and 7
                    (SOF received) set, PCI (bit 2) CLEAR. The schedule is
                    running -- USBCMD.RS was set in stage 5 -- and those two
                    are status-only here since USBINTR enables PCE alone. PCI
                    clear means the port-change condition was CONSUMED by the
                    handler, not merely counted.

  laststs=00000000  the LAST handler entry read USBSTS as zero. Expected for
                    the tail of a bounce burst: the NVIC latches a pending
                    interrupt while an earlier entry is still running, and by
                    the time that entry executes, the preceding one has
                    already W1C'd the status. Noted rather than explained
                    away; the load-bearing evidence is irqcnt with a real
                    connect edge, not this field.
