================================================================================
★ LOOKING AT A QEMU GOLDEN WITHOUT HARDWARE -- the reusable part
================================================================================

This tree's rule is that a golden is never recorded for a frame nobody has
seen: the knob pilot's arc clamp and the VGLite GPU frame were both perfectly
reproducible AND visibly wrong. Until now that eye-check needed the board.
It does not: QEMU's monitor will hand over the scanout buffer.

  qemu-system-arm -M mimxrt1170-evk -global fsl-imxrt1170.boot-xip=on \
      -kernel build/synthui_step_test.elf -display none -serial null \
      -monitor unix:/tmp/mon.sock,server,nowait &
  sleep 12
  printf 'xp/1wx 0x4080820c\n' | nc -U /tmp/mon.sock     # LCDIFv2 layer-0 ADDR
  printf 'pmemsave 0x80100040 0x384000 "/tmp/fb.raw"\n' | nc -U /tmp/mon.sock
  python3 -c "from PIL import Image; \
    Image.frombytes('RGB',(720,1280),open('/tmp/fb.raw','rb').read(), \
                    'raw','BGRX',2880).save('/tmp/fb.png')"

Two traps, both met:
  - ★ QUOTE the pmemsave filename. Unquoted, the monitor parses the path as an
    expression and answers "invalid char 't' in expression" -- while writing
    no file and returning nothing that looks like a failure to a script.
  - The monitor echoes every character back, so a naive capture buries the
    reply in terminal escapes; filter for the '^[0-9a-f]{8}: 0x' reply shape.

★ THE DUMP IS PROVEN TO BE THIS GOLDEN'S FRAME, not merely a frame: the
host-side FNV-1a over the dumped bytes computes 0xCE619CE1, exactly what the
firmware printed from its own framebuffer. That equality is what makes the
eye-check bind to the golden rather than to some other paint.

WHAT WAS CONFIRMED BY EYE, all sixteen reachable combinations in one frame:
  row 0: empty | gated | gated+accent | gated+slide | gated+accent+slide |
         cursor-on-empty | cursor-on-gated | everything
  row 1: the same eight, each additionally SELECTED
Every state was distinct and correct: accent is a round dot top-right, slide a
bar on the bottom edge, cursor a green ring, selected a white outline. The
cursor ring is drawn INSET precisely so it stays visible inside the selected
outline -- confirmed in row 1's last three cells, where both are on at once
(the playhead crossing the edited step, the one moment both matter).
