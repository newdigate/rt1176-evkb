------------------------------------------

   model with no partial-scanout timing.  Same scene, same animation, same
   eye, minutes apart: artefact present single-buffered, gone
   double-buffered.

2. THE REAL VSYNC/SHADOW-LOAD PATH WORKS AS MODELLED.  VSYNC_TIMEOUTS=0
   across 120 flips is the first silicon exercise of the INT_STATUS_D0
   polling path (lcdifv2VsyncArm/Seen): the real latch answered inside the
   40 ms bound on every single flip, at the panel's true 58.7 Hz — not the
   model's 60 Hz fiction.

   and both frame checksums (0x8CCC9E1D / 0x8BF7C89D) equal the QEMU run's

   firmware branched to TAP_ABSENT instead of asserting — the QEMU-only
   oracle stayed QEMU-only, per the standing asymmetry discipline.

What this run does NOT prove: the panel-scanned-A-then-B claim itself —
that is the tap's job and lives in transcript_qemu.txt; on silicon its
witness is the operator's eye on the intact, smooth sweep.
