waveform_draw_bench on the Nosh board
=====================================
Date:   2026-11-19
Board:  Nosh (second board), RK055 panel, landscape (rotated present), 32 bpp
Image:  examples/display/waveform_draw_bench/build-nosh/waveform_draw_bench.elf

DECISION: draw path C.
  The widget keeps the lane in its own strip (a ring in x; the CPU draws only
  the columns that scroll in) and copies it into LVGL's canvas with two PXP
  blits from the pre-flip hook. 30.2 fps, the pipeline's ceiling (baseline
  30.3; the first baseline run includes one 2 s start-up wait, ignore it).
  Rule: the first of A, B, C at >= 80 % of baseline and >= 20 fps.
  A (one lv_draw_rect per column, whole lane redrawn) reaches 13.7 fps: no.
  B (strip drawn with lv_draw_image) was not built: it is a CPU copy of the
  1.5 MB lane into SDRAM, slower than A's partial fill by construction.
  D-est (rotate the strip straight to the scanout buffer) gains nothing over
  C, so the LVGL port needs no new hook. The port's rotation of the lane
  costs 12.9 ms per frame and fits inside the 33 ms frame.
