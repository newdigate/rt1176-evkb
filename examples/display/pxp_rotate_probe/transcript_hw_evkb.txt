================================================================================
DECISION (plan Task 3 Step 3)
================================================================================
t_full = 13060 us > 5000, so NOT every-present-full: damage-grid present.
  c         = (t160 - t16) / (160*160 - 16*16) = 361 / 25344 = 0.014244 us/px
  threshold = floor((t_full - 4*t16) / c / 256) * 256 = 915456 logical px
            = 99.33 % of the 921600-px frame.
Reading: the rotate cost is linear in pixels and the per-op overhead is ~5 us,
so rects are cheaper than one full-frame op until the damage union is
essentially the whole screen.  A full present still costs 13 ms of a 33 ms
frame, which is exactly why the damage path is worth having; a knob's damage
(~160x160, cur+prev) costs well under 1 ms.  The start-up presents and PXP
error recovery remain forced full by design.
(The first boot's rect160 of 367 us would give 912896; the minimum over both
boots -- t_full 13060 from the first, rect160 366 from the second -- is used, as
min-of-8 is used within a boot.)
