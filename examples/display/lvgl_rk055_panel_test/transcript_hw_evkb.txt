Reproduction
------------

  pkill LinkServer; pkill redlinkserv; pkill crt_emu_cm_redlink
  cd examples/display/lvgl_rk055_panel_test
  /Applications/LinkServer_26.6.137/LinkServer flash MIMXRT1176:MIMXRT1170-EVKB load   build/lvgl_rk055_panel_test.elf
  /Applications/LinkServer_26.6.137/LinkServer flash MIMXRT1176:MIMXRT1170-EVKB verify build/lvgl_rk055_panel_test.elf
  python3 ../../../tools/rt1170-console.py /dev/cu.usbmodem<probe> 115200 &
  /Applications/LinkServer_26.6.137/LinkServer run MIMXRT1176:MIMXRT1170-EVKB build/lvgl_rk055_panel_test.elf &

  Do NOT hold the VCOM during load or verify (DAP status 131). There is no
  standalone `LinkServer reset` in 26.6.137; a backgrounded `run` is the reset.
