WHAT THIS PROVES
----------------

None of the following is reachable by ANY QEMU gate in this tree.  The
IW416 model returns zero scan results by design, so the board never
associates there and not one line of the accept path, the pool, or the
client data path executes.  The three gates prove bring-up, a real scan and
honest failure reporting; everything here is silicon-only.

1. ACCEPT PATH AND HANDLE LIFETIME.  `sess=3/87` after wifi_peer's `echo`:
   three sequential connections, 29 bytes each, every byte returned
   byte-exact (the peer FAILs on any mismatch).  lwip's tcp_arg points at a
   WiFiConnPool slot throughout, never at a WiFiClient.

2. TWO POOL SLOTS LIVE SIMULTANEOUSLY.  `sess=5/145` after `concurrent`:
   +2 sessions, +58 bytes.  That test stages bytes on BOTH sockets before
   draining either, and is demonstrated to FAIL against a 1-slot server.

3. THE EVICTION VALVE (design doc section 6), CONFIRMED FROM BOTH SIDES:
     - peer: `fill evicted_peers=1` -- it select()ed the four idlers and saw
       exactly one dropped by the board.  Eviction is tcp_abort(), which puts
       an RST on the wire, so this is the peer observing the mechanism
       directly rather than inferring it.
     - board: `evict=1` at alive=39, in the same window.
   Two independent observers, same count.  This test is demonstrated to FAIL
   (evicted_peers=0) against a stand-in that has room and never evicts, so a
   PASS means the valve fired, not merely that the 5th connection worked.

4. THE STALL VALVE, AND ITS TIMING MATCHES THE ARITHMETIC DERIVED IN REVIEW.
   `stall=3` first appears at alive=77, i.e. +38 s after the fill test's
   accepts at alive=39.  Task 9's code review derived the window by READING
   lwip: tcp_poll(..., 20) against TCP_SLOW_INTERVAL = 500 ms gives a 10 s
   poll cadence, and connPoll's body additionally requires > 30 s idle, so
   the abort must land in 30-40 s.  38 s is inside that window.
   The count closes too: `fill` leaves four idle connections, one was
   evicted (item 3), and the peer closing an UNCLAIMED conn does not free
   its slot -- connRecv's FIN path returns before touching lastActivityMs,
   so connPoll measures from the ACCEPT, not from the close.  4 - 1 = 3.
   Both the timing and the count were predicted from source and then
   measured.

5. WiFiClient COPY-ASSIGN AND COPY-CONSTRUCT.  `s_session = server.accept()`
   is a copy-assign and `serveEcho(c)` a copy-construct.  Until Task 9 these
   were absent from the linked ELF entirely (gc-sections dropped them,
   because -std=gnu++17 elides `WiFiClient c = server.available();`), so
   their refcount correctness rested on review and a reverted harness.  This
   run executes them.

6. THE SERVICE PUMP GENUINELY RUNNING.  Nothing echoes if it is not.  This
   matters because it is measured that the [wifi] QEMU gate is green with a
   working pump AND with a dead one -- a defect that permanently killed the
   pump shipped green through both gates during development and was only
   caught by instrumenting the image.

7. NO PATHOLOGY.  `refuse=0` throughout: the evictor always had a candidate,
   so no accept was ever refused.  No `echo_short=` line: the TX path never
   hit its 5 s no-progress budget, so no consumed byte was dropped.
   `srv=1/0` throughout: the listener stayed up with LISTEN_OK.


WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE
------------------------

* Throughput or sustained load.  Six sessions, 176 bytes.  This is a
  correctness run; m2_throughput_test is the throughput instrument.
* Behaviour on a healthy RF link -- see the bench caveat above.
* Accept refusal (`refuse=`) was never exercised: reaching it needs all four
  slots CLAIMED, which this sketch cannot do (it holds one session).
* The DHCP-timeout and reconnect paths -- see wifi_client_test's transcript.

THE ACCOUNTING, WRITTEN OUT SO IT VISIBLY BALANCES
--------------------------------------------------
1. BYTES.  sess delta = 6282-6 = 6276 sessions, 251222-182 = 251040 bytes.
   6276 x 40 = 251040.  EXACT, and it equals the peer's own independent
   count.  Every byte of 251040 made a full round trip through lwip and the
   radio and was counted on both ends.
2. THE VALVES, to zero residual.  1046 fills left 1046 x 3 = 3138 unclaimed
   PEER_CLOSED leftovers (4 idlers each, minus the one the valve evicted
   under the peer's eyes -- observed 1046/1046, fill_nodrop=0).  Their fates:
   evicted later by new accepts   4025(evict delta) - 1046 = 2979
   reaped by the stall valve       159 - 3(baseline)       =  156
   still live at the final sample  (pcb act=3)             =    3
                                                     total = 3138.  EXACT.
3. HEALTH: ONE distinct iw416 signature across 7221 samples --
     iw416 stranded=0 desync=0 split=0 drop=0 seqmis=0 pswake=0
   sort -u of every sample collapses to that single line: no counter was
   EVER non-zero at ANY sample, not merely clean at the end.
4. refuse=0 for the whole run, as this sketch's design requires (it claims
   no handle across passes, so the evictor always has a candidate).
5. LINK: zero wifi_status= lines in the soak window -- the association held
   240 unbroken minutes.  That crosses the ESP8266 softAP's default DHCP
   lease term with no visible renewal event and no interruption.
6. echo_short= never fired (the peer always reads; expected).

PCB PRESSURE (the headline number, previously a hypothesis)
-----------------------------------------------------------
MEMP_NUM_TCP_PCB=5, WIFI_MAX_CONNS=4, one-shot server = board holds every
TIME_WAIT (~120 s each).  Offered ~26 exchanges/min -- roughly 30x the
~2.5/min that five pcbs could sustain without reclamation -- for four hours.
Distribution over 7243 pcb samples (count, state):
   3016 act=3 tw=1   1777 act=3 tw=2   1002 act=2 tw=2   686 act=0 tw=1
    524 act=4 tw=0     70 act=0 tw=4     65 act=4 tw=1    53 act=0 tw=5
     19 act=5 tw=0     13 act=0 tw=3    12 act=0 tw=2      4 act=2 tw=3
      2 act=1 tw=1
act+tw never exceeded 5 (an honest-walk cross-check: the two lists share the
5-pcb pool), tw hit the full-pool 5 only inside drain windows and always
expired back down, and act=5 tw=0 moments (pool entirely active) recovered
without a single refused or failed connect.  CONCLUSION: lwip's
tcp_kill_timewait self-heal -- which announces nothing and increments no
counter -- sustained 30x the no-reclaim exchange rate for 240 minutes with a
byte-exact accounting.  The W22 handoff's headline risk is settled: the pcb
pool under this server's active-close design degrades NOTHING at bench rates;
it silently recycles TIME_WAIT and the accounting stays perfect.
