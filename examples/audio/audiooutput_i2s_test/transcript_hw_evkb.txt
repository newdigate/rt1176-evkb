Hardware transcript -- audiooutput_i2s_test on the MIMXRT1060-EVKB (rt1062).

Recorded 2026-08-13, branch rt1062-audio-phase5a, evkb 8efb28d, cores 5bcae78.
Board: MIMXRT1060-EVKB, DAPLink VCOM (/dev/cu.usbmodem14544402), console on
Serial6 (LPUART1).
Codec: WM8960 on LPI2C1 at 0x1A, driven by AudioControlWM8960.
Graph: AudioSynthWaveformSine 1 kHz @ 0.5 -> AudioOutputI2S (SAI1 TX DMA).

★ THIS FILE IS THE ONLY EVIDENCE THE TONE IS AUDIBLE. The QEMU gate's
STAGE_TONE is amplitude-only -- peak > 4000 over the raw tap, no frequency
analysis -- so it proves non-silent samples reached SAI1 TDR and nothing about
pitch or rate. If this transcript and the gate ever disagree, silicon wins.

TONE: audible -- steady 1 kHz from the board line out, confirmed by ear.
No dropouts or glitches reported.

setup() has long finished. The loop() health readout carries the evidence

- The Audio block pool lives in DMAMEM, which is UNCACHED on this board since
  cores a090c9d. This example is its first real exercise by the audio graph.
  The tone was clean -- the uncached pool keeps up at 44.1 kHz stereo.
