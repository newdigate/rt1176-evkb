dj_deck -- Nosh hardware transcript, 2026-11-19
Board: Nosh (second board), RK055 panel, SGTL5000, microSD on uSDHC2.
Image: build-nosh-bench (cmake -DDJ_BENCH=1): the app plus bench_step(), which
writes audio_codecs_probe's clips to /djbench on the card, deletes their
sidecars and loads each clip twice with no touch input.  The second load of
each must reuse the .apv/.asv/.att written by the first.

Load times (ms), loader slice DJ_LOADER_STEP_US=16000, screen paused:

  clip          audio  probe  decode  analysis  total   second load (sidecars reused)
  short.wav      2 s    175     694      375     1462    1047
  short.aiff     2 s    384     695      380     1679    1049
  click.flac     8 s    419    1973     1073     3722    1978
  click.mp3      8 s    486    2769     1075     4546    2953

Per second of audio that is about 0.25 s (FLAC) and 0.35 s (MP3) of decode,
so a five-minute MP3 takes about 1 min 45 s to load in full.  A track can be
played once its first second is decoded, and decode runs 3 to 4 times faster
than playback at unity pitch.

Slice tuning (plan step 4), same clips, first load:
  DJ_LOADER_STEP_US=5000, screen updated every loop:  flac 7708, mp3 11478 (slower than real time)
  DJ_LOADER_STEP_US=16000, screen updated at 5 Hz:    flac 3722, mp3 4546

Tempo: 120.00 BPM (2 s clips), 120.27 (FLAC, 16 beats), 120.26 (MP3, 17 beats);
the host engine gives the same tempo for each.

Boot finding: Nosh shares pad GPIO_SD_B2_05 between the flash read strobe
(FlexSPI1 DQS-pad loopback at 133 MHz, set by the boot header) and uSDHC2 CMD.
SD.begin() takes the pad and every later read from flash returns junk
(first seen as a UsageFault in SdFat's setSdclk, which this image keeps in
flash).  board_sd_begin() now moves FlexSPI1 to internal loopback at 58.7 MHz
from RAM first.  Firmware that runs wholly from RAM never notices.

NOT done on this board: nothing was touched by hand.  Play, pitch, scratch,
reverse, seek, cue and the browser are covered only by the QEMU gate and the
unit tests; nobody has listened to the output or tried the gestures on glass,
and no 3 to 6 minute file, over-length file or card-pull case has been run.
