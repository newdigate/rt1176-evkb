audio_codecs_probe on the Nosh board
====================================
Date:   2026-11-19
Board:  Nosh (second board), SW4 = 10, console J11 115200
Image:  examples/audio/audio_codecs_probe/build-nosh (audio-codecs local branch dj-deck, -Os)

WHAT THE DJ DECK'S LOADER SHOULD BUDGET, scaled from these clips to a 6 minute
44.1 kHz stereo track (the figures below are per 8 s, or per 2 s for WAV/AIFF):

  decode   WAV / AIFF   about 4 s      (2 s of audio in 10-20 ms)
           FLAC         about 22 s     (8 s in 0.49 s, 16x real time)
           MP3          about 47 s     (8 s in 1.05 s, 7.6x real time)
  spectrum              about 20 s     (8 s in 0.43 s)
  tempo                 about 4 s      (8 s in 82 ms, with the whole-track refinement)

  DECISION (plan rule: switch the FFT to CMSIS-DSP if spectrum + tempo for
  6 minutes exceeds 60 s): about 24 s, so audio-codecs' own FFT stays, and the
  .asv and .att files made on the board are byte-identical to the host's
  (asv_same=1 att_same=1 below).

  THE DECODER MUST LIVE IN DTCM.  With the D-cache off, where the decoder
  object sits decides its speed.  The same 8 s MP3 clip:
      object in SDRAM   40.2 s   (0.2x real time: a 6 min track = 30 min)
      object in OCRAM    9.0 s   (0.9x)
      object in DTCM     1.05 s  (7.6x)
  FLAC: 1.68 s in SDRAM, 0.49 s in DTCM.  The probe therefore constructs each
  decoder when needed in one 131 KB DTCM arena (ARENA line below); the deck
  needs the same.  -DPROBE_DECODER_IN_SDRAM=1 reproduces the slow figure.

  Memory to plan for: DTCM arena 131,104 bytes (the MP3 decoder; FLAC is
  98,848). Main-stack peak 66 KB (STACK_PEAK), nearly all of it one 64 KB
  array the FLAC decoder puts on the stack. heap_peak below (20 KB for 8 s) is
  only the heap's GROWTH, a lower bound; the tempo generator reserves about
  2 bytes per millisecond of track plus 21 KB for the refiner, so roughly
  100 KB for 9 minutes. NOT measured here on a long track.

Tempo: the board reproduces the host exactly (BPM, beats, and the .asv and
.att files byte for byte). The clip is a poor tempo target on purpose-built
grounds: each "click" is a 20 ms tone burst whose abrupt end is a second onset,
and it is 8 s long; the engine reads it as 120.27 (a 30 s version reads
120.01). Tempo accuracy itself is tested in audio-codecs with drum loops
(tests/test_tempo_refined_grid.cpp: 90 to 174 BPM within 0.02).

The decode loops in probe_decode.h are what the deck's loader should copy:
they stop at the length the container gives (tag chunks after the audio are
not played), start an MP3 only at a frame header that is followed by another,
and refuse MPEG-2 / 2.5 Layer III (the decoder gets those wrong).

MP3 decode equals the host's within 1 ppm per 4096-frame window and 0 LSB on
the excerpt, but is not bit-identical (a float decoder on a different CPU).
On the host the same decoder matches ffmpeg's decode within 1 LSB (78 dB SNR).
Its output starts with about 2257 frames of encoder/decoder delay and is 3168
frames longer than the source: gapless information is not read.
