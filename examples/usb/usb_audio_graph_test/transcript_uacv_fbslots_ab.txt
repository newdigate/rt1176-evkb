UAC2 feedback poll rate A/B: FB_SLOTS_HS 2 -> 8 -- 2026-08-05
==============================================================

The change the matched-soak baseline was built to license (USBHost_t36
f3f8817, pin bumped): the HS feedback reader goes from 2 iTD slots 16
frames apart (62.5 polls/s) to 8 slots 4 frames apart (250 polls/s) -- the
ceiling without growing the pool (ITD_POOL_SIZE 40 - 32-slot OUT ring = 8;
arming feedback now drains the pool to exactly zero). The EMA divisor
scales with the poll rate (uac1_fb_average now takes it explicitly: 8 at
62.5/s, 32 at 250/s) so both configurations hold the same ~128 ms filter
horizon -- 4x more reports into the same filter, never a shorter filter,
which is the guard against re-creating the +4.8 ppm dither-chasing bias.
FS reader unchanged (matched already; siTD pool has no room regardless).

Both soaks: EVKB host, UAC2/HS 8ch, 44.1 kHz, 20-minute window, judged on
repaired time. The A side is the corrected 2026-08-04 baseline; the B side
ran in pattern mode so R7 checked every sample while the loop was tighter.

Findings:
- 3.1x tighter. 296 B lands in the matched-cell band (macOS UAC2 holds
  204 B at 1000 polls/s): a 4x poll increase closed most of the measured
  4.5x gap. The residue vs macOS (~1.5x) is the same order as the FS
  servo-quality residue (196 vs 104 B), consistent with the baseline's
  second-order finding.
- The steady MINIMUM rose 392 -> 780 B: the excursions the old rate
  allowed were downward, toward dry-out, and they are gone.
- R7 green across the whole soak is the point of doing cooperative mode
  first: the tightened loop provably dropped nothing. Console showed the
  EMA target steady at +-1 mHz between heartbeats where the old rate
  wandered ~100 mHz.
- fb=44096214 -> the crystal still reads -86 ppm; W1 -0.025 ppm says the
  servo tracks it to the noise floor. No further rate-loop work is
  licensed by this data.
- wrap repairs: 174 phantom periods in this capture (the artifact scales
  with capture size); missing marks 0, packets 8000.6/s on repaired time.

Remaining from the tuning plan, in order of licensed value:
1. The ~1.5x envelope residue vs macOS at matched-ish polling -- servo
   quality (filter horizon shape, slew policy, packet-size choice), attack
   with an isolating measurement, not by turning knobs.
2. UAC1/FS: leave alone (+0.24 ppm, 196 B, matched).
3. 1000 polls/s would need ITD_POOL_SIZE growth; nothing in this data says
   the last 1.5x is worth 24 more descriptors.
