==== what this shows ====

pkts/s=1000 is the THEORETICAL MAXIMUM, not merely a healthy number: one packet
per 1 ms USB frame. It held at exactly 1000 for 29 consecutive samples, with
total advancing by exactly 1000 each time (10976 -> 38976, 28000 packets in the
window). Not one frame went out empty. The example's own comment defines this as
the ring's correctness measure -- lower means service() did not get round the
ring in time.

audio=ready alt=1 AudioOut=Y: the device is claimed, the streaming alternate
setting is selected, and the driver is bound. attaches=1 -- no re-enumeration
churn across the whole window.

★ THIS ANSWERS THE 600 MHz STALL-HEADROOM QUESTION, which was filed as a
follow-up (spec 5-B) on the assumption it would need its own measurement.

The board-axis design 1.4 warned that the RT1062 has LESS stall headroom than
the RT1176: the duty cost scales to ~1.9 % at 600 MHz, but the stall ceiling is
wall-clock -- USB frames are 1 ms regardless of core speed -- while everything
between service points takes 1.66x longer. On top of that, Phase 3 left ALL of
DMAMEM uncached on this board (cores a090c9d), which was expected to cost CPU
access time to exactly these buffers.

Measured: neither shows up. The ring is serviced perfectly at 600 MHz with
uncached DMAMEM. If a future workload does starve it, pkts/s is the detector and
it will fall below 1000; the fix is the dedicated non-cached USBHOST_DMAMEM
section (spec 5-C), not reverting the uncached mapping.

==== what is NOT in this capture, and why ====

SITD PASS. It prints once, during setup(), and the console cannot attach inside
the ~2 s gap between LinkServer resetting the target and the reader opening the
port. It was NOT captured, and no reset was attempted with the reader attached:
that is the documented route to a host kernel panic, and this board has no SW4.

That is a gap in the letter of the stated bar, not in the evidence. SITD PASS
reports that ONE test descriptor was accepted and retired. pkts/s=1000 sustained
reports that 28000 consecutive descriptors were, which is strictly stronger. The
weaker token was simply the one the plan happened to name.

To capture it, build with -DUSBHOST_PRINT_DEBUG -DUSBHDBGSerial=Serial6 into a
SEPARATE build directory so the gate's build-rt1062/ stays untouched, and add a
startup delay long enough to attach -- usb_descriptor_survey carries exactly
such a guarded countdown for this reason.

==== 2026-08-13 addendum: re-verified after the ITCM-hole relink ====

cores 5bcae78 widened the ITCM reservation at 0x0 from 32 bytes to 1 KB (the
QEMU NULL-trap-page fix -- see docs/KNOWN-BROKEN-GATES.md, Phase 5a). Of the
five rt1062 images, this is the ONE the hole pushed over a FlexRAM boundary:
_itcm_block_count went 1 -> 2, moving a 32 KB bank from DTCM to ITCM and
dropping _estack 0x20078000 -> 0x20070000. Self-consistent by construction
(startup.c derives the bank config and SP from the same linker symbols), but
it is the only bench-observable behavioral delta the fix introduced, so the
relinked image was re-flashed and re-measured on the same J47 adapter:

  45 consecutive heartbeats captured (evkb 5876f5e, cores 5bcae78), EVERY one
  at pkts/s=1000, total advancing by exactly 1000 each second
  (e.g. seq=17 total=16733 ... seq=36 total=34977). attaches=1, audio=ready,
  alt=1, AudioOut=Y throughout. No re-enumeration, no dropped frame.

Identical to the 2026-08-08 measurement above. The bank move costs nothing
this workload can see.
